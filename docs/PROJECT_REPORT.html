1. Executive Summary & Production Objectives

Modern enterprise organizations face immense challenges managing fragmented HR workflows: spreadsheet-based payroll prone to calculation errors, disconnected attendance trackers, subjective performance evaluations, and high employee attrition due to lack of predictive talent insights.

NexusHR provides a unified, production-grade enterprise workforce platform engineered to streamline the end-to-end employee lifecycle—from seamless digital onboarding to offboarding—while delivering automated statutory payroll calculation, contactless RFID biometric simulation, and AI-powered flight risk forecasting.

Key Challenges Faced & Technical Solutions

1. Statutory Tax Calculation Discrepancies:
Challenge: Tax deduction brackets and Provident Fund contributions often drift in manual spreadsheets.
Solution: Encapsulated salary calculation inside transactional service with standardized 40% HRA, 15% allowance, 12% PF, and tiered TDS deduction formula.
2. Low-Friction Demo Testing for Evaluators:
Challenge: Evaluators frequently encounter missing database credentials or expired third-party API keys.
Solution: Engineered dual-mode resilience: runs standalone with pre-populated rich enterprise dataset OR connects to live Spring Boot JPA backend without paid external keys.
